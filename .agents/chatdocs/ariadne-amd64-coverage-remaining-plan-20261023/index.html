Remaining coverage execution plan

Planning baseline: 2026-10-23, 08dcab92aabb1695c4f2b6748bb23f1ec822d72e. This continuation addresses the open instruction-coverage items from the current checkpoint. The canonical design owns the contracts. The HTML plan presents this section. Windows unwind work keeps its separate stage ledger.

The first delivery is a checked source-obligation ledger. The main promotion sequence is P0 → P1 → P2 → P3 → P4 → P5/P6/P7 → P8. The current native qualification N runs independently of the source audit and joins P8. Pilot implementation can start once its own source inputs are reviewed; it does not require waiting for every family. Full-stage completion requires the whole declared scope. No calendar estimate for full AMD64 is justified before P0–P3 expose the complete constraint and implementation worklists.

P0 — Baseline and obligations

Work. Freeze the intended AMD long-mode scope, selected publications, extension/mode/environment axes and required consumer set. Use the existing application and privileged/system scope; record the 24 extension-family discovery candidates and their supplementary-source decisions. Intel-specific forms need separate vendor dispositions. Scope exclusions must be justified before constructing domains, and later changes must be versioned visibly.

Create a stable obligation graph linking source cells, conflicts, table pairs, constraints, legal forms, XED relations, bindings and required validation. Seed it with all seven conflict records and all 33 table candidates. Distinguish accounted, adjudicated, implemented and validated; retain unresolved constraints even when their containing text is classified as non-encoding.

Files. Extend the existing source/geometry inputs. Proposed new files are tools/amd64_source_obligations.py, its tooling tests and reviewed decision inputs under tests/bap/fixtures/amd64-semantics/. These are planned files, not existing implementation. Keep generated ledgers and checker results under ignored target/; maintain schemas, source-decision inputs and small fixtures.

Exit. Every initial obligation has a stable ID, exact source references, dependencies, an explicit current state and an executable check to close it. Deletion, duplicate identity, wrong ownership and source/hash drift controls fail. A generated ledger cannot grant itself acceptance through stored flags.

P2 — Architectural constraints

Work. Add typed source-constraint and cross-reference extraction. Current region_accounting() only enumerates pages; source cells have unresolved footnote parsing. Replace that limitation with explicit obligations for:

  1. Prefix precedence/order/repetition, operand/address size, REX/high-byte registers and ModRM/SIB/displacement/immediate restrictions.
  2. VEX/EVEX/XOP fields, reserved combinations, CPUID/mode/privilege/OS-state applicability and feature-dependent aliases.
  3. Exception/outcome conditions, memory/order premises, diagram-derived effects and other selected architectural constraints.
  4. Footnotes, cross-volume references, opcode maps and supplementary sources.

Files. Proposed tools/amd64_source_constraints.py and tests; source-rule inputs under tests/bap/fixtures/amd64-semantics/; integrate with the corpus, geometry and obligation tools. Every typed clause retains exact source cells, its applicable owner/domain and referenced rules. Review the clause inventory independently from the parser's own output.

Exit. Every selected region and clause has a checked disposition, all required references resolve, and no required constraint remains unparsed. Omitted notes, swapped footnote targets, mislabeled diagrams, stale ownership and unknown clauses classified as harmless must fail. Reproduce the pinned corpus before check-products --require-all, then reconstruct geometry and regions. C1/C2 completeness requires this clause-level result as well as P1.

P3 — Legal domains and XED reconciliation

Work. Introduce a general domain/relationship path alongside the frozen v2/v3 REP registries. The existing enumerator handles finite literal prefixes, opcodes and independent fields; it has no general dependent ModRM/SIB grammar. Proposed modules are tools/amd64_domains.py and tools/amd64_reconciliation.py, with schema and executable tests.

Implement grammar in increasing complexity: finite prefix/opcode/register fields; dependent ModRM/SIB/address/displacement/immediate fields; then VEX/EVEX/XOP and applicability/alias predicates. Exhaustively enumerate small domains. For larger domains, add a restricted symbolic checker with explicit budgets and independently replayable witnesses/counterexamples. Unsupported constraints, timeouts and solver unknowns remain unresolved. Witness replay checks satisfying cases; it cannot establish that no hole or overlap exists. Bind each symbolic query to the grammar/translation, solver, compiler and configuration identities and execute it freshly. Require checked UNSAT certificates where supported, or explicitly review and state the trusted solver boundary. A self-reported absence of counterexamples cannot supply unqualified totality evidence.

Reconcile in both directions: each frozen XED candidate gets a source-backed relation, and every selected AMD legal form gets an obligation even when XED has no matching record. Relations may split, merge or alias domains. Keep encoding membership, architectural applicability and profile admission separate. Each candidate region needs an explicit in-scope legal, justified scope/vendor exclusion or unresolved disposition. Absence from the current manual-derived domain is unresolved unless authoritative evidence establishes exclusion or invalidity. Missing source coverage cannot shrink the denominator.

First vertical slice. Propose register-only byte MOV with opcodes 88/8A, ModRM.MOD=3 and no prefix or one REX from 40/41/44/45. The planned enumeration is 640 byte points: two opcodes × (64 no-REX + four × 64 REX combinations). Review Vol3 PDF282 source rows and prefix rules first. This exercises operand direction, extended registers, high-byte/REX low-byte aliases and partial writes through existing scalar state. It is a proposed finite slice, not an already qualified domain or a complete binding of either XED candidate. Preserve all other prefix regions as obligations. Follow with memory addressing and a source-reviewed feature-alias slice.

Exit. Source/domain membership, complete reconciliation and overlap/alias rules pass independent checks across the declared scope. Domain narrowing, omitted AMD-only forms, lost ModRM/REX combinations and unaccounted aliases produce concrete failures or explicit unknowns. Passing the first slice earns only that slice's result; full P3 requires the entire legal denominator.

P4 — Authoritative bindings and coverage reports

Work. Add a versioned general registry and report path without changing candidate-only coverage/v1 or the finite v2/v3 contracts. The current amd64_coverage.py deliberately reports zero authoritative full-domain bindings; do not replace that with a name-association count. Reuse strict identity/partition checking from amd64_legal_forms.py, but generalize rule dispatch, source derivation and required consumer/outcome capabilities.

Each binding connects a legal domain to a named semantic contract, exact producer/BIL query, implementation rule, profile/consumer set and current validation obligations. Require S ∪ U ∪ X = D with disjoint disposition partitions and binding-domain inclusion: S is an implemented named contract, U is explicitly unsupported and X is unresolved. A contract may include both normal and possible fault outcomes; those architectural alternatives are not the S/U/X partitions. Complete implementation separately requires S = D and empty unsupported/unresolved partitions. Architectural aliases and exceptions count as supported only through implemented contracts.

Exit. Every required domain has checked total accounting, and every supported partition has actual current producer and Rust/native observations. Replaying satisfying examples supplements domain checking; it cannot establish totality. Reject wrong bytes/extent/environment/VA/snapshot, stale source or producer, missing consumer capability, unjustified overlaps and solver unknown promoted to success. Report source coverage, authoritative domain bindings, implementation support and exercised validation separately.

N — Current v15 native qualification

Execute the prepared native stage in parallel once its existing source-handoff approval is obtained. The earlier automatic review rejected the changed 25-file payload; this plan does not change that recorded boundary. Revalidate the exact payload/current source, build a fresh core, audit fourteen native source hashes plus SDK/model/engine identities, and require 341 refusal controls, 448 native/Rust parity instances and the named compatibility regressions with the same helper.

Exit. Current-source native and local behavior match across the finite v15 domain, with final artifact/source stability checked. Close this baseline before promoting native REP proof-policy changes. N closes the existing finite gate; wider REP domains and consumers still have P6/P7 obligations.

P5 — Instruction-family implementation

Generate the implementation queue from P3/P4 gaps, distinguishing missing BAP lifts, transport types/resources, architectural state, projection/control and consumer support. Start the queue early; its complete acceptance depends on the completed denominator. Prioritize shared capabilities: scalar/control/flags, atomic and ordering behavior, x87/MMX aliases and FP state, wider SIMD/masks, then system/privileged/exceptional and remaining extension families. Retain every discovered family even when implementation is difficult.

Files. Pinned producers under native/bap/source-coverage/; transport/state and effects in src/bap/{ast,admission,registers,projection}.rs; control and matching native/checker modules as required. Wider vectors require typed state and alias contracts; raising a byte/tree limit alone cannot provide them. BAP supplies actual production semantics; LLVM remains decode/control reference.

Exit per family. Source legality → actual BAP BIL → named effect/control contract → required consumers → exact-byte and independent behavioral tests → current native parity are all connected. Missing lifts and generic unknown effects remain implementation gaps. An implemented conservative contract may retain justified environmental unknowns, with its invariant and limits tested.

P6 — Wider REP and fault/I/O outcomes

Implement separately versioned source-derived domains for prefix ordering, redundancy, segment selection and ignored/reserved combinations while freezing v12–v15. Preserve zero-iteration origins, weak writes, lane rules, overlap, iteration-dependent addresses, pointer wrapping and explicit exhaustion.

Before fault-sensitive or I/O admission, extend the outcome contract to normal completion, possible completed-iteration prefixes, faults and restart boundaries. Represent external I/O state and ordering explicitly. Coordinate transport, effects, investigation/report consumers and native replay; a normal-completion oracle cannot qualify partial effects. The current narrow terminal-control contract cannot simply be relabeled as general REP restart support.

Exit. Each required prefix/outcome domain passes legal-domain checks, fresh BAP and independent behavior checks, transport/refusal controls and current Rust/native parity. Fault and I/O domains need dedicated observers and fixtures that exercise partial completion and external side effects. Unsupported domains remain visible until their own gates pass.

P7 — Consumer contracts and proof admission

Freeze the shared loop/outcome premises with P6, then implement guard admission, captured targets, memory refinement and composition as independently versioned capabilities. Start with conservative whole-loop transfers: preserve entry origins, havoc every possibly written architectural/virtual binding, and treat possibly reached stores as barriers unless a checked footprint excludes them. Keep structural CFG edges and snapshot/query identity throughout.

Files and models. src/bap/{guard_admission,target_resolution,refinement,composition,core_protocol}.rs and matching native code; the owning predicate-admission, target-resolution, loop-target-closure, memory-refinement and guarded-target TLA+ models. Update input/effects/core models when their contracts change. The retired instruction-step ISA proof track stays retired.

Exit per consumer. Model checks, model-derived replay, actual Rust/native parity and saved-proof tampering pass under the new profile/receipt generation. Direct and framed entry points reject unsupported profiles, including empty and all-null domains, lookalike tokens and overall/site identity disagreement. Later-iteration writes, zero count and partial outcomes cannot inherit a first-iteration or normal-only proof. Whole-loop memory/target soundness must be exercised by behavioral mutations with unchanged observers.

P8 — Integrated full-coverage gate

Freeze the final scope, domains, source closure, producers, profiles, consumers and workload partitions. Run applicable affected tests and root formatting, default/core-only tests, strict Clippy and layout checks. Execute owning analysis-model checks, generated replay, compiled mutations, actual producer and native comparisons, and the declared capture/compatibility/resource suites. Keep each replay/mutation build in its own ignored directory.

Require a passing unchanged control before mutant credit; a changed SUT must compile and fail the intended unchanged observer. Recheck restored source, observer and helper identities. Missing prerequisites, skipped/ignored tests, solver unknowns, stale reports, timeouts and compilation failures supply no positive qualification credit. Maintain executable tests and small inputs; keep generated campaigns and acceptance outputs outside maintained source.

Exit. C1/C2 source and constraint completeness, C3 domain/reconciliation, C4 bindings, all required P5/P6/P7 capabilities and current N/P8 validation pass for the frozen scope. Required domains have no unsupported or unresolved implementation partitions. Only then may scoped full AMD64 coverage become true. The claim remains conditional on trusted BAP lifting, reviewed grammar/solver boundaries and stated environmental premises; it does not establish a universal ISA or Rust proof.

Delivery order, parallel work and checkpoints

  1. Next delivery: P0 ledger/schema and P1 review of all seven conflict records/five issues plus 33 table relations. Recheck official Vol4/5 sources in parallel; retain unresolved answers explicitly.
  2. Following delivery: P2 prefix/size/register constraint pack and the P3 byte-MOV pilot, each with exact source derivation and omission controls. Finish general constraint/reconciliation coverage iteratively by family.
  3. Next integration: P4 general binding/report path, preserving old reports and proving that partial pilots cannot increment complete candidate counts.
  4. Parallel execution: a source lane owns corpus/constraint tooling; a domain lane owns grammar/reconciliation/bindings; runtime lanes own producer, state, REP and consumers in disjoint modules. N uses an isolated native build. Coordinate shared outcome/schema changes before integration and serialize native source freezes and final qualification.
  5. Every completed slice: preserve exact scope, executable regressions and counter deltas; update the canonical design, this ledger and CHECKPOINTS.md. Re-run source-bound binding campaigns after relevant source changes. Publication follows the user's commit/push instruction for that delivery.

The plan can be reviewed and implemented in these bounded deliveries. Seven adjudicated conflicts or a passing 640-point pilot must never be reported as completion of the entire source or AMD64 domain.
