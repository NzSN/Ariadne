Completing REP and AMD64 operation coverage

Context and follow-up

Status. Completion design with finite source/domain implementation, 2026-10-23. The working tree based on 6c3fa96 implements separately selected v12/v13/v14 REP scopes and an AMD-specific v15 address-size successor, a candidate five-volume source corpus, historical-gap accounting and v2/v3 finite legal-domain/binding registries. The dated stage execution distinguishes exercised clauses from the remaining full-stage exits. The broader encoding grammar, source completeness and consumer expansions remain open; the finite plain-dependency profiles do not grant proof-consumer admission. The v15 producer, projection and local capture gates pass; its current native core handoff is awaiting specific approval after automatic review rejected the changed payload.

Why this document exists. The current BAP integration owns the original three-form v12 contract; this design records its separately versioned memory-string successors and remaining obligations. The origin checkpoint records the starting 10,569 unbound symbolic candidates and 2,657 manual extraction gaps. Geometry now gives all 2,657 old gaps source-backed dispositions, including explicit source conflicts; none lacks a disposition. Neither the candidate count nor a transcription-gap count is a count of unsupported legal AMD instructions.

What this document establishes. This is the canonical design for closing source, encoding-domain, implementation and consumer coverage. The BAP design continues to own the implemented semantic interface and profile contracts. This document owns the completion data model, algorithms, dated finite implementation findings and remaining exits.

Where to go next. Read the completion stages for execution order, the test matrix for executable validation, and the BAP trust boundary for what testing can establish. The execution HTML is the current dated reading view; the original proposal preserves the pre-execution version.

What remains unresolved. The legal-form denominator, source conflicts and unparsed constraints, wider REP semantics, architectural state and proof consumers remain incomplete. Official byte identity for the pinned AMD Volumes 4/5 is unverified. Universal ISA execution correctness and Rust refinement remain separate obligations. Windows unwind coverage remains with the investigation design.

1. Problem and completion target

The work has three distinct deliverables:

  1. A complete, source-backed inventory of legal forms within an explicit architecture scope, with checked encoding domains and exclusions.
  2. Versioned bindings from those domains to implemented BAP transport, projection and consumer contracts.
  3. Executable validation of every required form and contract partition, with gaps, tool failures and environmental premises remaining visible.

The proposed initial source scope is documented AMD64 long mode, including application and privileged/system operations, the five architectural subsets already named by the program, and an explicit extension manifest. Each release must freeze vendor, publication revisions, extensions and execution modes. Intel-only, future and undocumented XED records retain explicit dispositions; they are not silently promoted into the AMD denominator or silently dropped. A combined AMD/Intel claim needs its own pinned Intel source and extension set. The scope freeze is an implementation gate, not a scope decision already accepted by this proposal.

All documented forms inside the selected scope remain obligations. Missing capture state, privilege, CPUID or operating-system state can produce explicit conditional outcomes; they cannot justify removing a difficult instruction from the denominator. A later scope change creates a new manifest and a visible delta. It cannot turn an old incomplete campaign into a complete one.

“Full coverage” here means implementation and validation coverage of that declared domain. It does not mean every concrete instruction execution has been tested or that BAP has an independent ISA-correctness proof. A finite sample cannot establish those stronger claims.

3. Architecture

The source lane establishes encoding membership and applicability. The semantic lane implements analysis behavior under pinned BAP lifting. Their join is a versioned binding registry; the coverage validator checks their common domains and current validation inputs. XED and LLVM provide independent encoding and control checks. Neither supplies a substitute instruction-effect implementation.

Maintain source pins, compact grammar/rule definitions, reviewed correction patches, generators and small regressions in the repository. Reconstruct PDFs, full layout/geometry products, expanded inventories, solver products, reports and copied build trees under ignored target/ or temporary work. Do not restore a retained qualification-evidence collection.

4. Source reconstruction and manual gaps

4.1 Freeze a reproducible source corpus

The implemented candidate source-corpus/v2 lock identifies publication, revision, publisher, retrieval origin, exact PDF size/digest, selected chapters, appendices and global encoding-rule obligations. The source-corpus tool binds extractor sources, executable and shared-library hashes, versions, options, locale and normalization schema. Its decision remains candidate-proposed-unreviewed; 24 source-listed extension-family candidates are discovery inputs with explicit unresolved supplementary-publication and CPUID/vendor audits. A live URL alone is not a reproducible source identity.

Preserve the existing corpus lock. A new extraction toolchain produces a separate candidate lock. Review PDF identity, full-text/geometry differences, source-region coverage and normalized row/constraint differences before adopting it. Reusing the old content digest is insufficient if changed source regions were never represented by the old extractor.

4.2 Recover geometry and ownership

Use PDF word/glyph coordinates, table boundaries and outline ownership to build source cells. A sourceCell has a PDF digest/page, rectangle, glyph span/digest, table/row/column identity, owning instruction sections, continuation links and footnote references. Printed page labels are useful annotations; PDF page indices remain unambiguous source addresses.

The parser must track tables across pages and represent merged cells explicitly. A spanning encoding cell can cover several operand rows only when geometry and source structure justify that span. Never fill down a previous opcode merely because the current cell is blank. Preserve alternative interpretations as unresolved records.

Every old gap receives a source-backed disposition: resolved cell, justified continuation, non-encoding material, duplicate/alias, or unresolved. Record the original gap ID, affected source cells, correction rule and review rationale. Deleting a gap, suppressing a line class, or moving text outside a table does not resolve an obligation. The implemented geometry extractor now preserves those identities and reconstructs word-coordinate ownership; its finite execution leaves ambiguous cells, cross-page continuations and unparsed constraints open.

4.3 Establish source completeness separately

Account for every selected source region, including prefix rules, operand-size rules, exception/applicability notes, appendix maps and extension updates. “All 2,657 gaps resolved” closes the old transcription backlog only. It does not prove that the previous 4,118 rows contained all instruction forms or all global constraints. Source-region coverage and legal-form derivation require their own reviews and executable omission controls.

5. Legal forms and authoritative bindings

5.1 Keep identities separate

These records define the completion target. The restricted legal-forms/v2 pilot and profile-bindings/v2 pilot now implement a finite subset; the wider source/domain and semantic-contract records remain to be completed:

Entity Required identity and content
CoverageScope Vendor/publication/extension set, modes, environment axes, required semantic profiles/consumers and digest.
SourceCell Exact source location, ownership, transcription/constraint role and reviewed disposition.
LegalForm Source references, operation/operand roles, byte grammar, effective-size rules, prefix constraints and applicability predicates.
TemplateRelation Legal-form IDs, frozen XED IDs, relation kind and source-backed explanation for equivalence, splitting, aliasing, overlap or absence.
SemanticContract State reads/writes, normal/exceptional outcomes, memory/order/environment effects and conservative-analysis obligations.
ProfileBinding Legal-form domain, producer and implementation rule IDs, semantic-contract ID, profile/consumer capabilities, source/ABI digests and validation obligations.
ByteCase Exact consumed bytes, decode environment, binding/form IDs, independent expectations and current observations.

A manual row can describe several legal forms, and several rows can describe one form. One XED template can intersect multiple forms, and several templates can cover one form. Store these relations explicitly; mnemonic and IFORM name agreement is only a search aid.

Source-backed AMD forms absent from XED remain first-class obligations. Their absence triggers a decoder/producer gap or a reviewed alternate encoding reference; it must not remove them from the legal inventory. Conversely, each XED candidate needs an explicit vendor/scope/alias/undocumented/future/unresolved disposition.

5.2 Specify a restricted encoding grammar

Represent consumed instruction bytes, limited to 15 bytes, with declarative constraints over opcode maps, prefixes and ordering, operand/address size, REX/VEX/EVEX/XOP fields, ModRM/SIB, displacement, immediate and extension fields. Preserve operand roles and register restrictions, including high-byte/REX conflicts and reserved fields. Stream bytes after the consumed instruction are separate input, not part of its identity.

Keep these predicates distinct:

encoding_member(form, bytes, decode_environment)
instruction_applicability(form, architectural_environment)
profile_admission(binding, consumed_bytes, decode_environment,
                  pinned_producer_identity, typed_BIL, requested_consumers)

A recognized encoding can require an architectural exception in a particular environment. It is not necessarily undecodable. Feature-dependent opcode aliases need guarded resolution; a disabled feature must not automatically be treated as either “not in scope” or a generic invalid opcode. Admission must validate the entire chain from captured consumed bytes through the legal-form match to the exact producer query/BIL and consumer selection. Bind snapshot/VA, bytes/extent, target/environment, helper/runtime identity and AST digest; a well-typed BIL shape for different bytes is insufficient. When a feature-dependent alias cannot be selected from justified premises, retain the alternatives under a reviewed join or report the unresolved applicability.

5.3 Check totality and overlap

For a legal form f, let D(f) be its declared byte/environment domain. Each point must have a checked disposition: implemented binding, architectural alias or exception contract, explicit unsupported obligation, or unresolved region. Aliases and exception outcomes count as supported only through their own implemented contracts. Scope exclusions are accounted before constructing D(f); removing a point already inside it requires a new reviewed scope manifest.

The validator checks:

S(f) union U(f) union X(f) = D(f)       # total disposition accounting
S(f) = D(f), U(f) = X(f) = empty       # implementation completeness
binding_domain is a subset of its referenced legal-form domains
overlaps have identical meaning or a checked, source-backed dispatch rule

Here S is the union of implemented binding domains, U is explicitly unsupported, and X is unresolved. Covering a region with an unsupported record completes its accounting, not its implementation.

Use finite enumeration for small fields and a restricted constraint solver for larger symbolic products. A solver timeout, unsupported constraint or unknown answer leaves the obligation open. Do not assign success after a work cap. Independently replay satisfying witnesses and counterexamples; use separate checks for table/grammar derivation, partition coverage and implementation dispatch. The grammar and its translation remain reviewed trust premises.

A byte recipe is a witness to membership, not proof of totality. A collection of passing examples cannot certify S(f) = D(f). Conversely, domain totality does not prove instruction execution semantics; that remains under the BAP trust boundary and separately exercised analysis contracts.

5.4 Version the authoritative path

Keep ariadne.amd64-form-case-associations/v1 candidate-only and preserve coverage/v1 behavior. The separate v2 legal-domain checker implements legal-forms/v2, profile-bindings/v2 and coverage-assessment/v2 for the restricted pilot. It rejects unknown fields, duplicate identities, orphan references, inconsistent aliases and stale source, producer, grammar or consumer digests. Unsupported grammar and exhausted enumeration return an unknown outcome; the pilot supplies no wider solver or source-completeness claim.

No authoritative: true switch may promote an existing association. The new checker must validate the source derivation and domain obligations. Digests bind the reviewed inputs; hashing a claim does not establish its authority.

6. Remaining REP design

6.1 Expand operation dimensions explicitly

The proposed memory-string progression is:

Family Repeat condition Element widths Address-size work
MOVS, STOS, LODS F3 count-controlled 8 / 16 / 32 / 64 Default 64-bit addressing, then separately reviewed 32-bit addressing.
CMPS, SCAS F3 while equal; F2 while not equal 8 / 16 / 32 / 64 Same independent address-size progression.
INS, OUTS Separately reviewed F3 I/O repetition 8 / 16 / 32 Separate external-state, privilege and exceptional-completion contracts.

R1 implements the default-address portion through two successor profiles: v13 for 24 nonempty original producer lifts, and v14 for those forms plus four LODS lifts from a separate BAP source bundle. See the dated execution below for producer, projection and consumer-validation scopes.

These family/condition/element choices give 28 memory-string planning tuples at default address size, including the current three, or 56 after adding the second address-size choice. They are planning tuples, not legal-form or exhaustive-encoding counts. Prefix/segment variants and environmental domains still require source-derived bindings. AMD repeat-prefix tables are the source starting point.

Each recipe records operation, element width, address/count/index width, repeat condition, applicable source-segment selection, exact prefix constraints and environment predicates. A MOVSD string encoding must never be selected by the ambiguous mnemonic alone. Extra prefixes, prefix order, repeated prefixes and feature modifiers remain unreviewed until their particular domains are established; F2/F3 never become generic permission bits.

For comparison/scanning repetition, the newly produced ZF controls continuation after the first iteration. Tests must distinguish an initial ZF from the first comparison's result. Element size and address/count size are independent. Intel's repeat-operation reference provides an independent primary cross-check.

6.2 Preserve the abstract loop contract

Retain the v12 zero-or-more dependency fixed point as the minimum conservative contract. Entry origins survive the zero-iteration alternative; possible body writes do not become definite kills. LODS/SCAS add accumulator lane and flag obligations. Element-width changes add multi-byte memory, overlap and arithmetic wrap cases. Address-size changes add count/index lane rules and upper-half behavior; test nonzero upper halves with both zero and nonzero effective counts. Do not infer zero-count upper-lane behavior from a generic register-write rule.

Recognize a reviewed producer loop structure with bound temporaries, typed conditions and no unreviewed external control. Do not synthesize effects from an opcode when BAP lacks the lift. A larger legitimate BIL tree needs a reviewed transport/resource contract; raising an AST limit alone does not implement a missing architectural state bank.

Preserve bounded work and explicit exhaustion. A more precise range or closed-form optimization must conservatively refine the established loop summary and pass the same zero-iteration and loop-carried-origin controls. Do not concretely iterate a captured RCX value: the snapshot does not establish the instruction's entry count or earlier executed iterations.

6.3 Separate normal completion and fault/restart semantics

Extend the semantic outcome model before claiming fault-sensitive REP support. Represent normal completion, a possible completed-iteration prefix followed by a fault, and interruption/restart boundaries under their architectural premises. Do not make a partial prefix imply the final normal result or attach a normal-completion guard to a fault outcome. Memory accesses need conditional width/progress/ordering information, not a first-iteration affine address reused for the entire operation.

I/O repetition requires explicit external I/O state and side effects; it cannot be implemented by a memory:any rule. A port read can precede a later memory fault. The Intel REP INS reference and comparison fault behavior must be reconciled with the selected AMD contracts before admission. A normal-completion numeric oracle does not close those gates.

6.4 Version every consumer capability

Freeze v12's current meaning. Allocate successor semantic profiles and, where needed, new receipt/checker generations; numeric version IDs are assigned at implementation time. Reusing the same producer binary does not make a new projection or proof contract equivalent to an old one.

Consumer Required expansion contract
Plain dependency projection Exact recipe admission, bounded closure, lane/flag/memory effects and explicit outcomes.
Address evidence Retain every possible access; iteration-dependent addresses stay unknown unless a separate range contract is validated.
Guard admission Derive transfers over the whole loop; preserve an unaffected register only after checking all writes; no assumption of nonzero count or normal completion.
Captured targets Havoc every possibly written binding or validate a loop summary; preserve original edges, candidates and coverage obligations.
Memory refinement Treat all potentially reached writes as barriers unless a checked footprint proves exclusion; no first-iteration disjointness shortcut.
Composition/native replay Replay the same profile, outcomes, query and proof premises; invalidate receipts when any bound rule changes.

Use explicit capabilities per profile and consumer. During migration, a consumer continues to refuse unsupported recipes before work, including empty and all-null source domains and direct checker entry points. Bind the overall semantic identity as well as site identities. Old/current transport mixing and substring lookalikes must remain negative controls.

7. Close the rest of AMD64 by contract family

After the denominator is available, generate work by missing capability rather than selecting convenient mnemonic examples. BAP source gaps, transport gaps, state-bank gaps, projection gaps and consumer gaps are separate classifications. PUSHFQ's AST-budget failure belongs to transport/producer assessment until a reviewed lift is transported; it does not count as supported after a counter is increased.

Architecture-state stages need explicit alias and partial-write rules for wider vectors/masks, x87/MMX relationships, control/status state, segment bases, system registers and external state. Atomic/order effects and system or exceptional control require contracts beyond ordinary scalar writes. Coarse state:other writes cannot justify killing unrelated aggregate origins.

Each family slice must connect source legality, actual producer BIL, normalized effects, required consumers, independent tests and an exact profile identity. An implemented conservative contract may legitimately retain environmental unknowns. An unimplemented operation cannot close its row by emitting generic unknown effects. BAP remains the semantic dependency; LLVM remains the independent decode/control reference. Supplied LLVM IR and abstract stateflow remain separate APIs.

8. Validation and acceptance

Track four independent axes for every binding:

Axis States and required evidence
Source/domain Unresolved, reviewed member/alias, justified exclusion; source-backed grammar and totality/overlap checks.
Implementation Missing producer/transport/state/projection/consumer, or implemented named contract; no credit from decoding alone.
Execution Unexercised, passed, failed or blocked by prerequisite; exact current source, tools, profile, query and corpus.
Capability Explicit support/refusal for each required consumer and outcome; approved conservative behavior has a stated invariant and precision limit.

Do not overload one Boolean “supported” with all four meanings. Report source coverage, binding-domain coverage, tested partitions and semantic outcomes separately. A full claim names its scope and required consumer set, and requires all of their obligations. A narrower plain-projection release can state its own finite support while program-wide Q1 remains open.

The validation suite must include:

  • Independent source-region omission and merged/continued-cell regressions; legal AMD forms absent from XED; overlapping feature-dependent aliases.
  • Exact-byte positives and negatives for widths, address size, prefix fields, register restrictions, reserved encodings, length boundaries and trailing stream bytes; wrong-byte/environment/producer bindings despite valid typed BIL. Unsupported is distinct from architecturally invalid.
  • REP zero/one/many counts, both directions, initial/opposite ZF, first/late mismatch, overlap, pointer wrap, lane preservation, segment-state uncertainty, later-iteration writes, virtual bindings and resource exhaustion.
  • Actual fresh BAP BIL with independently supplied numeric/flag/memory expectations, Rust/native full-state comparisons and saved-proof tampering.
  • Compiled mutants for dropped source regions, narrowed domains, omitted entry joins, single-pass loop summaries, stale output equations, first-iteration alias exclusion and omitted overall-profile checks.
  • Applicable analysis-model/replay checks and root format/default/core-only/ strict-Clippy/layout checks. New analysis contracts need their owning model changes and qualification; an encoding inventory does not replace them.

Mutant credit requires a passing exact control, a compiled changed SUT, the intended unchanged observer's behavioral rejection, private build outputs and restored source/observer checks. Missing runtimes, solver unknowns, timeouts, compile failures, ignored tests and old reports never supply positive credit. Keep a small actual regression for each closed failure class; regenerate the large campaigns instead of maintaining their logs or passing flags.
